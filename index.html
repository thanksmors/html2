A field kit for self-contained pages

Offprint — pages that stand alone

Self-contained .html pages you can restyle, edit, and rearrange. Every file carries its own theme engine, editor, and components — no build, no server, no network. Open one, make it yours, and when it's right, flatten it to a plain static page. Inspired by Anthropic's "unreasonable effectiveness of HTML".
